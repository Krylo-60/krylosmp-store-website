Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about KryloSMP.

🎮 How do I join KryloSMP on Bedrock (Mobile / Console / Windows 10)?
Enter Server IP: krylosmp.falix.gg and Port: 29273 (or default Bedrock port 19132). Floodgate and Geyser cross-play will automatically connect your client without requiring a Java account!
🪙 What are KryloCoins (KC) and how do I earn them?
KryloCoins (KC) are the 100% Free Play-to-Earn currency of KryloSMP Season 1. You earn coins by playing on the server, mining, participating in BoxPvP tournaments, claiming daily rewards, and winning community events. Type /balance to see your coins, and /kcshop to spend them on Extra Hearts, Crate Keys, and VIP Ranks!
🎨 Why is my custom skin not showing up?
Because our server operates in cross-play mode, skins are managed via SkinsRestorer. Simply type /skin <username> in game chat to load any skin instantly (or /skin url <link> for custom NameMC/Imgur skins)!
📜 How do I view Server Links in-game?
Press Esc ➔ Server Links... in Minecraft 1.21+ to open our official menu, or type /store, /website, or /discord in game chat.
